Privacy Policy

HPQ Scavenger Hunt Discord Bot • Owner / Organizer: HopocketQu33n
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the HPQ Scavenger Hunt Discord Bot (“the Bot”) collects, uses, and protects information when operating within Discord servers.

2. Information Collected

The Bot may collect and store the following information:

  • Discord user IDs and usernames
  • Event submission metadata (timestamps, streamer references)
  • Moderation actions related to submissions

3. Information Not Collected

The Bot does not collect:

  • Private messages
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Payment or financial information
  • Activity outside designated event channels

4. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Operate and manage community events
  • Verify and moderate submissions
  • Generate leaderboards and event results
  • Maintain fairness and prevent abuse

5. Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell, rent, or share collected data with third parties. Data may be viewed by authorized event moderators solely for event operations.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained only for the duration necessary to operate the event and resolve disputes. Event data may be archived or deleted at the organizer’s discretion.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored data. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Organizer Authority

HopocketQu33n retains full authority over all stored data, including the ability to modify or delete data in accordance with event operations.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the organizer through official HPQ Discord channels.
